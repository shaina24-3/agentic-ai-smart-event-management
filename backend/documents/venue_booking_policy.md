# Venue Booking & Availability Policy

## 1. Venue Allocation Guidelines
- **Single Occupancy Rule:** A physical venue or auditorium can host only ONE event at any given time slot or date. Double booking is strictly prohibited by system validation rules.
- **Advance Reservation:** Venues must be booked at least **3 days** in advance to allow time for technical setup, AV check, and security verification.
- **Operating Hours:** Campus venues are available from **08:00 AM to 09:00 PM**. Late-night events after 09:00 PM require special administrative approval from facilities management.

## 2. Available Venues & Seating Capacities
- **Grand Ballroom A:** Seating capacity of **200 seats**. Located at Hotel Plaza, Floor 1. Equipped with dual projection and banquet seating.
- **Tech Innovation Hub:** Seating capacity of **50 seats**. Located at Silicon Tower, Floor 4. Ideal for hands-on technical workshops, high-speed Wi-Fi, and workstations.
- **Main Amphitheater:** Seating capacity of **500 seats**. Located at University Campus, Sector C. Suitable for keynote addresses, conferences, and large summits.
- **Auditorium Alpha:** Seating capacity of **100 seats**. Located at Building A, Tech Park. Suitable for seminars and department demos.

## 3. Venue Conflict & Overbooking
- If a user or organizer requests to book a venue that is already occupied on that date, the AI Assistant and backend must immediately issue an availability conflict alert and recommend alternate open venues or alternate dates.
