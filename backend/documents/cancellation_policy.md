# Event Cancellation & Refund Policy

## 1. Participant Cancellation Policy
- **Cancellation Window:** Registered participants can cancel their registration up to **24 hours** prior to the scheduled event start time without any penalty.
- **Refund Policy:** If the event has a registration fee, 100% refund is processed within 3-5 business days for cancellations made before the 24-hour deadline.
- **Late Cancellation:** Cancellations made within less than 24 hours of the event start time are non-refundable and marked as late cancellation.
- **No-Show Policy:** If a participant does not attend without prior cancellation, their account may be restricted from registering for high-demand workshops for 30 days.

## 2. Organizer Event Cancellation Policy
- **Organizers' Rights:** Event organizers with ADMIN permissions reserve the right to reschedule or cancel an event due to unforeseen circumstances, severe weather, or speaker unavailability.
- **Notification Requirement:** If an event is cancelled by the organizer, all registered attendees must be automatically notified via email and system notification at least **48 hours** in advance whenever possible.
- **Automatic Refunds:** In the event of organizer cancellation, 100% of registration fees (if any) are immediately refunded to all participants.
