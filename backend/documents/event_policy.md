# Event Management Policy

## 1. Scheduling Guidelines
- Events must be scheduled at least 48 hours in advance.
- All events must specify an approved venue, date (YYYY-MM-DD), start time (HH:MM), and capacity.
- Double bookings of any venue for overlapping time slots are strictly prohibited by system validation.

## 2. Capacity Enforcement
- Total confirmed registrations cannot exceed the maximum capacity assigned to the event.
- Event capacity cannot exceed the architectural capacity of the host venue.
